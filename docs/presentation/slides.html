01 · The problem المشكلة

People get sick and don’t know what to do.

Is it serious?

People can’t tell whether their symptoms point to a disease, so they worry, or they wait too long.

Which doctor?

Even when they decide to go, they don’t know which specialty they need, and lose time and money.

Far from home

When they travel, they don’t know where the nearest doctor or hospital is.
