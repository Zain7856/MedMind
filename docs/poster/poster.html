Your Health • Our Priority

Check on your health with an AI assistant, learn about diseases, and find and book a doctor or hospital, without leaving home.

01 · WHYPeople get sick and don’t know what to do.

The problem المشكلة

Sick, and not sure what to do

Many people feel unwell and can’t tell if it is serious. They go to a doctor, lose time and money, and often don’t know which specialty they needed.

Our solution الحل

Check, learn, then book

MedMind lets people check their symptoms, read about diseases and ask an AI assistant, then book the right doctor or hospital online.

02 · SEE ITFrom a symptom to a booking.من العَرَض إلى الموعد

Symptom checker: four symptoms ticked (cough, fatigue, fever, headache). Seasonal Flu has all 4, so it comes first, then COVID-19 with 3.

AI chat: a signed-in patient asks about a headache and a mild fever. Gemini answers in plain words and says when to see a doctor.

  • A disease library20 diseases, each with an overview, a treatment and its symptoms.
  • A symptom checkerTick any of 31 symptoms. Diseases come back ranked by how many match.
  • Doctors and hospitals15 doctors in 15 specialties and 10 hospitals, with place and fee.
  • Book in one stepPatients pick a date and time. The doctor or hospital confirms it.
  • An AI assistantSigned-in users ask Gemini in their own words, up to 1000 characters.
  • Admin toolsApprove or reject new doctors and hospitals. Add, edit and delete diseases and symptoms.
