How it works

Three steps, in the order people actually use them.

  1. 1

    Narrow down what it might be

    Open the disease library and pick a condition. Its page carries an overview, the symptoms recorded against it and treatment notes, so you read one condition in full before you talk to anyone about it.

  2. 2

    Ask the question in your own words

    Describe what you are feeling and the assistant answers in plain language, keeping the thread of the conversation as it goes. It also points you towards whether this is something for a doctor, a hospital, or neither right now.

  3. 3

    Book with the right person

    Browse the directories to see each doctor's specialty, location and fee or each hospital's location and services, then send a booking request from their page. Requests and their status live in your profile.
